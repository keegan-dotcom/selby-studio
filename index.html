What is known

Disclosure means a government saying openly what it knows about UFOs, now officially called UAP.

On the record

  • The US military has studied these objects since 1947.
  • Three Navy videos are authentic and still unidentified.
  • Officers have sworn that a crash retrieval program exists.
  • The House has voted for a review board with subpoena power.

Still unproven

  • That any object is non-human technology.
  • That any craft, material or body is in government hands.
  • Every retrieval account so far is testimony.
  • The official position is that there is no evidence of visitors.
