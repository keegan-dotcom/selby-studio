01 / STUDIO

The studio model, at family scale.

A venture studio doesn't wait for pitches — it originates its own companies. We run that model with the smallest possible team and the fastest possible tools.

№ 01

Own the whole stack.

Strategy, design, engineering, commerce, and support all live under one roof. Nothing is outsourced, so nothing is lost in translation.

№ 02

Ship in weeks, not quarters.

AI-native workflows mean an idea can become a live, revenue-ready product in days. We use that speed to test in the market, not in meetings.

№ 03

Every venture launches with working commerce and a price. Products earn their keep from day one — or teach us something and step aside.

No headcount, no burn, no board. Staying small keeps every product personal — built the way we'd want it built for us.
