02 · Start here

What is known

Disclosure means a government saying openly what it knows about UFOs, now officially called UAP. Here is the ground nobody disputes.

12,618reports logged by the Air Force's Project Blue Book, 1947 to 1969. It left 701 unidentified.
143 / 144military reports the US intelligence community could not explain in its 2021 assessment.
1,652reports held by the Pentagon's UAP office by late 2024. It said 21 new cases had no answer.
34officials who went on camera in 2025 to allege a hidden recovery program.
5batches of government UFO files published since May 2026 under a presidential directive.

On the record

  • The US military studies these objects and has since 1947.
  • Three Navy videos are authentic and still unidentified.
  • Officers have testified under oath that a crash retrieval program exists.
  • Congress has voted for a review board with subpoena power.

Still unproven

  • That any object is non-human technology.
  • That any craft, material or body is in government hands.
  • Every retrieval account so far is testimony. No hardware has been shown.
  • The official position is that there is no evidence of visitors.
